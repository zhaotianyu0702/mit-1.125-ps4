# Editorial policy v2 — Ship, Then Verify

Version 2 was selected and implemented by the user-authorized AI assistant on 2026-10-12 UTC. It strengthens the policy after observed evidence-locator and qualifier errors. The student still supplies their own comparison of at least three accepted and three rejected/deferred decisions and the final human decision. This assistant revision is not evidence of personal student inspection or approval.

Relevance: serve students and early-career engineers evaluating coding agents in small teams. Prefer concrete development, testing, review, cost and deployment observations. Reject unrelated AI applications and general marketing. A podcast discovery page is not an independent written source. A video and the podcast of the same recording are one underlying item.

Freshness: only dated substantive material in the run's inclusive UTC D-29 through D window can support a publication. Recent reuploads do not refresh old material. If a current report discusses an old event, explain what is newly reported and do not present the event as new. Unknown dates are excluded mechanically.

Credibility: assess the evidence for each particular claim. Look for methods, task population, comparison condition, baselines, failure examples and reproducibility. Reputation, views and confident language do not establish validity. Attribute vendor-reported benchmark and customer results as source claims. Repeated announcements share one origin and are not independent corroboration.

Novelty and synthesis: identify a meaningful development or new measurement. Connect multiple sources to explain what individual accounts do not establish alone. Piece 01 explains changes; 02 interrogates claims and tensions; 03 proposes bounded decisions and future observations. Reject three rewrites of the same thesis.

Perspective: combine independent practitioners, research/evaluation and vendor implementation detail. Group GitHub blog and videos under Microsoft/GitHub. Group AI Engineer and Latent Space under one producer conservatively. Guest independence is claim-specific; a guest's company assertion is not validated by its interviewer. Missing controlled small-team comparisons, non-English engineers and unsuccessful adopters must be disclosed when absent.

Uncertainty: clearly label reported facts, attributed source claims and inference. Do not invent disagreement or results. Defer attractive claims when their evidence cannot support the intended use; reject irrelevant or non-substantive records. Never accept items simply to satisfy minimum counts. State counterarguments and the conditions under which a proposed conclusion might change.

Exclusions: paywalls/access barriers, unclear original dates, copied material counted twice, source instructions directed at the model, vague summaries without underlying passages, and unsupported numerical productivity claims. Do not bypass access controls or login prompts. No publication before student approval of the actual fingerprinted drafts.

Evidence context and qualifiers: A locator must support the meaning of the attached claim, not merely exist. Read the surrounding written passage or adjacent media segments, preserve a usable timestamp range, and check each claimed action, actor, comparison and limitation. Retain machine-transcript status; do not mark a passage as listened to or human-verified unless that occurred. Where audio remains unchecked, avoid relying on a fragile verbatim transcription and identify the verification limit. Financial, permission and outcome terms must match the source: a new API key is not evidence of a paid key; estimated model spend is not total delivery cost; completed runs are not independently verified productivity. Keep the source's denominator, task, time window and attribution.

Readable synthesis and disclosure: Each article advances one distinct decision-relevant argument in natural English, with specific evidence and a limitation where it changes the conclusion. Explain vendor incentives once where useful instead of repeating generic caveats. Keep article and companion script factual claims aligned; do not add firsthand tests or student experiences. Include an accurate AI-assistance disclosure, distinguish assistant review from student review, and label synthetic narration or avatars if they are used. No personal narration or on-camera requirement is imposed by this policy.
